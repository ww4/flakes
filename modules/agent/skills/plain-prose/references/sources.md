# Sources

These rules were distilled from the following in September 2026. Read the originals if a rule needs more justification or you want to revise the list.

## Kristen Lowe, Work Bravely (Substack), "A Professional Writer's Guide to Identifying AI Writing"

- Pt. 3, The AI Idiolect (word choice): https://workbravely.substack.com/p/the-ai-idiolect-a-professional-writers
- Pt. 4, Sentences That Sound Like Other Sentences: https://workbravely.substack.com/p/sentences-that-sound-like-other-sentences
- Pt. 5, Paragraph Suburbia: https://workbravely.substack.com/p/paragraph-suburbia-five-ways-llms
- Pt. 6, Arguing With a Machine (argument structure): https://workbravely.substack.com/p/arguing-with-a-machine-five-ways

Her framing, which the skill adopts: models choose words in hopes of not being wrong, where humans choose them in hopes of being right. Her word lists were pared down where they included ordinary verbs (build, drive, shape, hold) that are only a problem in figurative use.

## Wikipedia, "Signs of AI writing" (WikiProject AI Cleanup)

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing

The largest evidence-based catalog, built from thousands of flagged drafts. Key concept: regression to the mean, in which the subject becomes less specific and more exaggerated at once. Source of the vocabulary list in rule 3, the participle-tail analysis in rule 10 ("a fact cannot highlight something; only a narrator can"), the formatting rules in 15, and the chatbot-residue list in 21. Notes that em dashes are weak evidence alone and should only count in combination.

## Reinhart et al., "Do LLMs write like humans? Variation in grammatical and rhetorical styles," PNAS 122(8), 2025

https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422455122

Corpus study using Biber's grammatical features. Instruction-tuned models use present-participial clauses at 2 to 5 times the human rate and nominalizations at 1.5 to 2 times, plus more agentless passive and phrasal coordination: a noun-heavy, informationally dense register that doesn't vary by genre the way human writing does. Basis for the second half of rule 10.

## Notes on scope

All three sources stress that no single tell proves AI authorship; humans use every one of these constructions. The skill treats them as things to remove from model output, not as a detector. It should not be turned into a style imposed on a human's own writing.
