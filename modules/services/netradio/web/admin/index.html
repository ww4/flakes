Specialty feeds

A feed is a title and a description. Add one and the agent reads the description and builds the rule (artists, genre words, instrument measures) from what the library holds; edit the rule by hand any time. A listenable feed is a station of its own.

Schedule

Themed segments and spotlights on the curated stations. A slot plays a feed, an artist (with similar artists we own mixed in), or auto — the DJ picks a compatible feed or artist for the day, a different one each time. Only feeds and artists that fit the station are offered. The DJ announces the day's schedule at breaks.

Curated stations: name, which families they are (what the schedule may offer them), and the base rule they play outside segments. Specialty stations come from the feeds above.
